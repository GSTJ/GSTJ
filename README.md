# Gabriel Taveira

Engineering Lead with over nine years building mobile, web, AI, and developer-tooling systems. I've worked from early-stage products to platforms at Coinbase, Meta/Kustomer, D-ID, AB InBev, and Morgan & Morgan.

I lead teams and still stay close to the code. Security is part of that work: threat modeling, identity and authorization, secure file handling, CI/CD hardening, and software supply-chain controls.

## Security engineering

- [Reproduced and closed a stored-XSS path](https://github.com/GSTJ/gabriel-taveira-portfolio/pull/163) at the R2-to-site asset boundary.
- [Made OTP consumption atomic under concurrency](https://github.com/GSTJ/pegada/pull/181), with 25 regression races proving one success and one rejection.
- [Bound signed image-upload grants to the requesting user](https://github.com/GSTJ/pegada/pull/180), including exact byte-size signatures, expiry, quotas, and cleanup.
- [Hardened shared tooling trust boundaries](https://github.com/GSTJ/magic/pull/10) against symlink races, mutable CI dependencies, and unverified release archives.
